# Safety switches (per run, per pool)
Opus keeps one tally per pool: Hydrogen-built tasks and OpenCode-built tasks reviewed, and how many failed their FIRST review (re-reviews do not change it). A task counts for the pool that built most of its files (tie: OpenCode). Flash-built tasks (rare last resort) and Argon-built tasks are not tallied.
- From the 3rd reviewed task of a pool on, after each first review: if MORE than 1 in 3 of that pool's tasks failed their first review (failed x 3 > reviewed), the pool is **tripped** for the rest of the run.
- A tripped pool gets no NEW tasks. Tasks already building finish and are reviewed as usual. Opus prints "Safety switch tripped: <pool>, <failed> of <reviewed> failed their first review" in the plan and records it in `.m11/last-run.md`.
- **Hydrogen tripped:** Routing 6 and 7 go to OpenCode `build` directly (and its steps in fix ladders are skipped). **OpenCode tripped:** it takes no new builds (they stay on Hydrogen) and its fix steps are skipped; scouting, special input and the `hard` fallback are unaffected. **Hydrogen tripped and OpenCode drained, or OpenCode tripped and Hydrogen drained, or both tripped:** the non-design builds go to Argon medium; Argon drained -> `flash-high` for exact-spec mini-tasks only, otherwise held, ask the drained-pools question (`.claude/m11/drained-pools.md`).
- Scouting is not affected. The tallies and switches reset on the next run.
